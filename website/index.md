## Why Phaxa?

Phaxa is designed for corporate environments where "black box" agents aren't enough. By combining **Temporal.io** for reliability with the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** for connectivity, Phaxa allows you to build workflows that are both autonomous and auditable.
